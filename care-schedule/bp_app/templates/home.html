About us

Booking a doctor shouldn't take a week

Most clinic bookings still run on phone tag - you call, they call back, that slot is gone.

CareSchedule puts the professional's real calendar in front of the patient. Healthcare professionals publish the times they are actually free, and those exact slots are what patients see and book. No waiting list, no double bookings, no "we'll get back to you."

Everything stays in one place afterwards. Patients can reschedule or cancel on their own, and both sides keep a record of past visits — so the next appointment starts with context instead of guesswork.
